Defending against deep fake news with large language and image models

generated image

Generative AI tools are used more and more for solving complex problems. However, they may be exploited for generating deepfakes. These are typically associated with disinformation campaigns. In this context, deep fake news refers to news content generated using AI models. It may cover complete generation and/or manipulation of existing data (such as face swap).

This platform was created within the DeepNewsDef project. It is intended to check news content (text and images) to determine if it was created and/or manipulated using generative AI models (large language and image models).

Project results are made publicly available on the project website, GitHub, and through scientific publications.
